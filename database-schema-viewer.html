Day 1 Architecture Milestone

Food Delivery Platform Schema & ER Diagram

Comprehensive database model powering the three interconnected portals: Customer Ordering, Admin Platform Management, and Delivery Partner with Map Routing & OTP verification.

🛍️ Customer Portal

Browses restaurants and menu_items. Creates orders and saves addresses. Receives the auto-generated 4-digit delivery_otp to share with the rider.

🛠️ Admin Dashboard

Onboards and manages restaurants and categories. Manages dish prices and in-stock toggles. Monitors platform orders and advances status from PLACED ➔ PREPARING ➔ READY_FOR_PICKUP.

🛵 Delivery Partner Panel

Toggles status (ONLINE/OFFLINE), accepts orders ready for pickup, navigates via interactive route map using restaurant & customer coordinates, and completes delivery via delivery_otp verification.

🔐 OTP Delivery Verification

A 4-digit code generated on order creation. Customer sees it on tracking; Rider must input this OTP to transition the order to DELIVERED.

🗺️ Map Coordinates

latitude and longitude on both restaurants and customer addresses feed directly into the Leaflet routing map.

🧾 Price Snapshots

order_items.unit_price captures the historical price at the moment of checkout, ensuring past receipts never change.
